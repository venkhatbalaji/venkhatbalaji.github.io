My Introduction

I’m Venkhat Balaji, a Lead Software Engineer based in Abu Dhabi. At Finstreet, I lead backend architecture for regulated exchange, wallet, custody, and Private Funding Platform workflows aligned with FSRA and ADGM expectations.
